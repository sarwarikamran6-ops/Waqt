Waqt privacy

Waqt is a paid app. The Google Play price for Canada is CA$1.99 before GST/HST.

Location is used only to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction. The place you pick is stored on the device. Prayer checkmarks and favorites stay on the device.

Quran text, translations, and hadith are loaded from the public jsDelivr copies of the fawazahmed0 datasets. City search uses Open-Meteo. Those requests include the query or chapter you open. Waqt has no account and does not sell data.
